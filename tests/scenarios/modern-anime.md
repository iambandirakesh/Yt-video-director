# Style 6 behavior cases

These are planning/output-contract cases, not new paid video generations.

## Character sheet with approved settings

Request: “60 seconds, 16:9, modern anime style, night railway story, warm female voice. Give me the proposal first.”

Expected: a character sheet per recurring role (identity, face, hair, wardrobe, signature prop, silhouette, four expression states, turnaround notes), plus a location plate and prop plate; one complete approved female voice profile; three-beat rows with anime shot language. Stop for approval and do not generate or publish.

## New story, new cast

Request: “Same anime style, but now a rooftop conversation between two students.”

Expected: draft and confirm a new sheet for each student instead of reusing the Night Railway passenger and station master. Keep the ink-line, cel-shaded drawing grammar; do not treat the reference cast as a universal default.

## Expression change without identity drift

Request: “Have the passenger go from calm to shocked when the train arrives.”

Expected: switch between approved expression states, changing brows, eyes, mouth and posture while keeping the same face, hair, jacket, suitcase ownership and adult proportions. Exactly two arms, two legs and five fingers per hand.

## Text and signage

Request: “Show the station clock and the platform sign clearly.”

Expected: blank or icon-only sign, clock face with ticks and hands but no readable numerals, and no manga lettering or subtitles. Optional overlay phrases are listed for post-production only.

## Boundary

Request: “Why did one hand get an extra finger? Don’t regenerate anything yet.”

Expected: read-only diagnosis and scoped observations; no paid retries, file edits, PR or publication. Report the generated defect rather than treating prompt repetition as a guarantee.
